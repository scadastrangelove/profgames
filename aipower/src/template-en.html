AI stack: four structures of power at one checkpoint

How to read

Structural power is not a command; it is the ability to set default conditions. Here it appears through access to compute, models, data, capital and critical decisions.

Anti-overclaim rail

Weakly sourced or disputed items are kept visible, but marked by status, confidence and caveats.

Story map

Dots are facts positioned by event date on one shared scale. Colored bands show each arc's factual span; lines on the right connect arcs to thesis nodes. Claims, caveats and graph-service links are not drawn as events.

Capital: not one ranking, but different money regimes

Annual flows, private investment, capex, cumulative state-guidance funds, sovereign programs, mobilization targets and pledges are different accounting regimes. The included announcement total is useful as a map, not as a spend total.
