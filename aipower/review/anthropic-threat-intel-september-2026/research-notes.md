# Anthropic threat intelligence, September 2026

Cutoff: 11 September 2026. Base: AI Power Atlas v0.34 at `9fba416831a22ed713a5872d5d841e9f87cb6cc0`.

## Selection

The report was not imported as one omnibus fact. Seven records were retained because they add distinct mechanisms to existing arcs: provider observability/access control, three operational cyber workflows, state surveillance, influence-production infrastructure and a paired U.S.-China dispute over distillation.

Conventional-weapons and biological-misuse sections remain deferred. The report-wide statement that sophisticated operations no longer require sophisticated attackers remains Anthropic's assessment, not a representative incidence claim.

## Source roles

- Anthropic is the primary provider-telemetry source for the cases and disruption actions.
- Microsoft independently describes the related CaptiveCrunch campaign and significant AI support. It does not independently establish Anthropic's detection-triggered rebuild loop.
- NSA, CISA and FBI provide the U.S. official position on distillation.
- China's Ministry of Commerce provides the Chinese official response. Neither official position independently audits the full traffic corpus.
- Axios is secondary coverage with a provider interview on state-surveillance cases.
- Habr supplies the Farrell/Newman panopticon/chokepoint interpretation. It is an analytical frame, not factual corroboration.

## Overclaim controls

- The report explicitly selects notable and novel cases; it is not a prevalence sample.
- “Possible zero-days” are not converted into CVEs or independently validated vulnerabilities.
- “Agents performed nearly all of the work” retains human target selection, monetization choices and review.
- Provider visibility is bounded by traffic and evidence available to the provider and partners.
- Detection, account bans and intelligence sharing do not create public-law authority.
- Production apparatus is separated from measured persuasive, electoral or policy impact.
- February and September distillation metrics cover different windows and populations and are not summed.

## Structural-power mapping

The report gives an unusually direct observation of two mechanisms co-located in hosted infrastructure: the ability to correlate part of a flow and the ability to terminate access. The atlas therefore maps the evidence beyond the cyber tab into access control, sovereign flow gating and cognitive security, while preserving the limits above.
