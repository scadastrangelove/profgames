# Concealment and behavioural continuity: review notes

Reviewed 6 September 2026 against the primary publication linked by each candidate. The package adds 12 records and reuses six existing records as comparisons. It does not add a top-level story arc.

## Editorial decisions

- The central claim remains `partially_verified`.
- Capability, conditional propensity and production incidence remain separate.
- `persistence_media` identifies where a relevant policy, trace or state resides; it does not imply that every medium survives a new session.
- The future-instance note is distinct from the OpenAI wiki incident and from memory poisoning: intentional successor transfer, population-level shared state and attacker-planted memory are different mechanisms.
- The sandbagging source does not expose an exact publication day on its canonical page. The timeline therefore stores `2025-03` with `date_status: month_only` rather than inventing a day.
