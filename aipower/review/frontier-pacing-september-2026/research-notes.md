# Frontier pacing and sovereign control

Research cutoff: 14 September 2026. Base atlas: v0.35 at commit
`4ac9115f2b3257c188920eb078f1c4cdb1b7d85c`.

## Finding

The September debate is not one fact about whether AI should move fast or slow. It exposes
two competing control surfaces:

1. Frontier laboratories control training cadence, release timing, model versions and access.
2. The national-security state controls procurement, export rules and the conditions under
   which a supplier may retain or lose access to public contracts.

The Pentagon's January strategy and NSPM-11 make the second surface institutional rather
than rhetorical. The OpenAI pause makes the first surface operational rather than merely
hypothetical. Amodei's proposal tries to turn private pacing into a verifiable governance
regime; Trump's response rejects the proposed direction but does not create a new legal
instrument.

## Evidence discipline

- The Department of War memorandum is a binding internal strategy, not a market-wide law.
- NSPM-11 is binding executive direction inside the national-security enterprise. It does
  not create a general right to seize a private model.
- Amodei's essay is a first-party policy proposal. Only the embedded-evaluator commitment
  is unilateral and immediate.
- Altman matched the evaluator commitment. Musk and Hassabis endorsed the direction but
  did not publish equivalent operating commitments.
- Trump's statements are recorded as political positioning. Their institutional antecedents
  are represented separately by the January strategy and NSPM-11.
- The existing `export-14` claim remains the legal counterweight: a district court vacated
  one challenged supply-chain-risk measure, so state checkpoint power is not unlimited.

## Structural-power mapping

- Embedded evaluators: inward observability into training and safeguards.
- Capability checkpoints: a release and training-tempo gate.
- Chip, distillation and weight controls: external chokepoints intended to preserve room
  for asymmetric pacing.
- NSPM-11 continuity clauses: a reverse chokepoint limiting unilateral supplier shutdown.
- Modular architecture and multiple vendors: route-around capacity and partial exit.

## Exclusions

The package does not convert Amodei's six-to-twelve-month botnet scenario into a forecast
endorsed by the atlas. It does not infer regulatory capture, a cartel, a common training
schedule or implemented international coordination from public statements.
